PHONE NUMBER REQUIREMENTS

Business Phone Numbers & Regulatory Requirements

If your AI voice solution requires a new business phone number, the number provider may require business, identity, address, registration, or other regulatory information before the number can be provisioned.

What you should be prepared for

  • Requirements vary by country and by phone-number type.
  • The provider may need to verify the actual business or end user of the number.
  • Depending on the country and number type, information may include business name, business address, registration details, authorized representative information, identity or supporting documents.
  • Some countries require local address or other local eligibility conditions for certain number types.
  • Approval and number availability are determined by the telecommunications provider and applicable regulations.

United States

U.S. number requirements depend on the number type and intended use. Phone-number regulatory registration is generally different from messaging registration, and additional business or compliance information may apply. We will confirm the exact requirements during the provisioning process rather than assuming that every U.S. number has the same requirements.

United Kingdom

UK local and national business numbers have specific regulatory requirements. For business customers, information can include the business name, registration authority and number, website address, business address, authorized representative details and business classification. Certain UK number types may also require eligibility conditions or supporting information.

Number types we normally provide

Our standard service is focused on local and national business numbers where available and compliant for the customer's business. If you require a toll-free, premium, short code, or another special number type, you may purchase that number directly in your own account, and we can guide you through the technical integration process.

Requirements, availability, pricing and approval are always subject to the telecommunications provider and the applicable country's rules.

Regulatory information can change. The information above is provided as general guidance and does not replace the current requirements shown by the phone-number provider for the selected country and number type.
